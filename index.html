On-Demand Technician Dispatch • Jamshedpur

On-demand doorstep repair connecting households with technicians. Engineered 60s mobile booking, QR partner security, 4-digit OTP dispatch, and transparent ₹99 inspection pricing.

Next.js Supabase Firebase Auth ₹99 Model

01. PROBLEM & FIELD FINDINGS

Household repair in Tier-2/3 cities suffers from fragmented local technicians and hidden charges. Field research across Jamshedpur sectors (Sakchi, Bistupur, Sonari, Kadma) proved customer preference for a flat ₹99 doorstep inspection fee waived upon job execution.

02. USER FLOW

OTP Phone Auth → Service Selection (AC / Plumbing / Electrician) → 60s Checkout → 4-Digit OTP Technician Dispatch → Job Completion Sync.

  • Boundary Validation: Handled negative & extreme inspection fees (e.g. ₹0 or > ₹50,000 limits).
  • OTP Expiry & Form Resubmission: Verified edge cases during network drops and unverified OTP resends.
  • UI & Mobile QA: Tested mobile booking responsiveness across 10+ viewport sizes.
